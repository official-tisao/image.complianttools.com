# P4-21 benchmark corpus notes

This directory contains the benchmark index and per-capability evidence. The status of each register entry is summarized in [`p4-21-corpus-index.md`](p4-21-corpus-index.md); absence of a measurement is explicitly recorded as partial, blocked, or not applicable.

Measured assets and outputs currently present:

- Four individually CC0-verified image sources in `fixtures/cc0/`, with API/source snapshots and content hashes.
- T32 Tier 1 results and 20 hashed PNG artifacts in `t32/`, plus four x2 model PNG results; CPU conversion/parity and the same-corpus metrics are recorded in `t32/conversion/cpu-parity-report.json` and `t32/model-results.json`.
- All 163 recursive official `tessdata_fast` tree entries are registered in `docs/static-assets.json`: 123 language/variant binaries, one deprecated alias pointer, 37 script models, and two helpers (`equ`/`osd`). The initial eight-language browser OCR measurement and offline limits are recorded in `p4-21-t62-ocr.md` and `../ocr-language-smoke-results.json`.
- T66 has an initial generated-fixture comparison of five local methods in `t66/results.json`; the corpus limitations are explicit in `p4-21-t66-inpaint.md`.
- Two owner-approved official Real-ESRGAN source checkpoint entries in `docs/model-assets.json`; both convert to ONNX and pass CPU parity, and the x2 model passes a tiny-input Chromium WASM smoke. x2 does not improve this synthetic interpolation corpus; broader degradation comparisons remain open.

No reference/model output is claimed unless it is generated by a verified pipeline and recorded with input provenance, runtime/version, method, and hash. Remaining unmeasured benchmark entries are listed in the index; do not mark P4-21 complete while any required capability remains without its appropriate measured entry.
